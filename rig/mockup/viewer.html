    Notes for the printer

    1. Real parts in matte black. The amber here only marks what gets printed.
    2. Print thread_coupon first: the adapter's M67 × 0.75 thread on a 2mm grip ring. It tells us if the thread fits before anything bigger is printed.
    3. Print the adapter flat, thread axis vertical, at 0.12–0.16mm layers.
    4. The pressure plate and wedge plate print on their bottom edges, so the pad and ramp slopes are traced in each layer, not stepped.
    5. Not in the CAD yet: leaf springs, plate guides, wedge plate grooves, detent bumps, the knob's snap, and how the lid and dock fasten.
