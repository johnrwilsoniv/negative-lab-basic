Notes for the printer

  1. Real parts in matte black. The amber here only marks what gets printed.
  2. The 0.75mm thread pitch is fine. Print the adapter flat, thread axis vertical, at 0.12–0.16mm layers.
  3. Print the adapter first. It's small and fast, and it tells us if the thread fits before anything bigger is printed.
  4. The lock clamps by about 0.1mm more than the film is thick, which is what grips it. The plates flex to take that up. If it locks too hard or too soft, change the ramp rise, not the channel.
  5. Print the knob separately and snap it onto the tab after the wedge plate is in. Add a small detent bump at each end of the slot so the switch clicks into SLIDE and LOCK.
  6. Largest part is the light dock, about wide. That fits a 220mm bed.
