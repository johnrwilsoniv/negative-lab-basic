NLB Scan Rig

A 3D-printed, light-tight rig for scanning a whole uncut roll of 35mm negatives with a camera. A Sony A7 with a Laowa 90mm 2X macro looks down a baffled tube into a film gate over a CineStill CS-Lite. The roll runs from one spool to the other through a fixed channel in the gate, and two sprockets drive it by its perforations: one turn of the knob is one frame. This design has not been printed yet.
